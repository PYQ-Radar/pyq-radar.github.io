Basic troubleshooting

1. Refresh: Press Ctrl + Shift + R to reload the page without using the cached version.

2. Check connection: Open another website to confirm your internet is working.

3. Sign in again: If account data does not load, sign out and sign in again.

4. Try another browser: This helps identify browser-specific problems.

5. Share useful details: Include the page URL, device/browser, and exact error text in your report. Never share your password or verification codes.

Account safety: Never share your password, one-time verification code, or private account credentials in a problem report.
